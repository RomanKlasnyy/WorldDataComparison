WorldDataComparison — third-party data notice
=============================================

This application bundles or derives statistical data from the sources below. Upstream
terms apply to the data; the application code is separate. Source-level licence and
redistribution flags are stored in config/sources.yaml and shown in Data quality → Sources.

This project is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
sponsored by the World Bank, the International Monetary Fund, the United Nations,
Our World in Data, Transparency International, V-Dem, or any other data provider.
Trademarks and series names belong to their respective owners. Figures are provided
as-is for analysis; verify material claims against the upstream source.

World Bank — World Development Indicators and Worldwide Governance Indicators
  https://data.worldbank.org/
  https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/worldwide-governance-indicators
  Licence: CC BY 4.0. Attribution: World Bank.

International Monetary Fund — World Economic Outlook (via IMF DataMapper)
  https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/WEO
  Licence: IMF terms of use, free with attribution. Attribution: International Monetary Fund.
  Only historical years are imported; WEO projections are excluded.

Our World in Data
  https://ourworldindata.org/
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 for OWID processing; upstream series keep their own terms.
  Attribution: Our World in Data, based on the upstream sources listed per indicator.

  Indicators delivered through OWID and their upstream producers:
    Human Development Index, expected years of schooling, Gender Inequality Index — UNDP
    Mean years of schooling — UNDP / Barro-Lee
    Liberal and electoral democracy indices — V-Dem Institute
    Corruption Perceptions Index — Transparency International
    Press Freedom Index — Reporters Without Borders
    Self-reported life satisfaction — World Happiness Report / Gallup
    Voter turnout — International IDEA / V-Dem
    Homicide rate — UNODC
    Median age — UN World Population Prospects
    Working hours — Penn World Table

  V-Dem data is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0; Transparency International's CPI has
  its own attribution and reuse terms. Review those terms before redistributing a public
  bundle that includes these series.

Not bundled (manual import only, not redistributed):
  OECD PISA, Freedom House, Yale EPI, ND-GAIN, IEP Global Peace Index,
  Social Progress Imperative, and the EIU Democracy Index (commercially licensed).
